About Herger's & Co.

A Nigerian law firm built on constitutional principle, statutory precision and a track record of results for clients across twelve practice areas.

Our History

Our Story: From a Single Office to a Full-Service Practice

Herger's & Co. was established in Lagos by a small group of litigators and transactional lawyers who shared a conviction that Nigerian businesses were too often advised by counsel who treated the country's statutory framework as an obstacle to be worked around rather than a foundation to be built upon. The firm's founding partners had cut their teeth at the Federal High Court and before the Securities and Exchange Commission, and they built the practice on a simple discipline: every position taken, in negotiation or in court, must be traceable to a specific constitutional provision, statute or regulation.

That discipline shaped the firm's early growth. Within its first years, Herger's & Co. moved from general commercial litigation into specialist mandates in taxation, mining and capital markets, as clients returned not merely for representation but for the firm's habit of anticipating regulatory change before it affected their businesses. As Nigeria's 2007 legislative wave reshaped entire sectors — the Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act 2007, the Federal Inland Revenue Service (Establishment) Act 2007, the Merchant Shipping Act 2007, the Investments and Securities Act 2007, the NIMASA Act 2007, and the Public Procurement Act 2007 among them — the firm built dedicated practice groups around each new framework, rather than folding the work into a generalist commercial team.

Today, Herger's & Co. operates twelve specialist practice groups spanning mining and production law, taxation and fiscal policy, shipping and international trade, regulatory compliance, white collar defence, real estate, capital markets, privatization and venture capital, mergers and acquisitions, media and entertainment law, litigation and alternative dispute resolution, and corporate governance. Each group is led by a partner with sustained, sector-specific practice experience, supported by associates trained in the firm's foundational discipline of statutory precision.

Our growth has been organic and client-led. We have never pursued scale for its own sake; every new practice group and every lateral partner hire has responded to a demonstrated client need, and every mandate — from a first-time entrepreneur's company incorporation to a multinational's cross-border acquisition — receives the same rigour applied at the firm's founding.

Constitutional Alignment

Grounded in the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria

The 1999 Constitution is the supreme law of Nigeria, and every opinion Herger's & Co. issues is consistent with it — not merely with convenient practice.

Supremacy of the Constitution

Section 1 of the 1999 Constitution establishes its supremacy over every other law in Nigeria, including any Act of the National Assembly. We test every statutory interpretation against this hierarchy before advising a client.

Rule of Law & Fair Hearing

Sections 35 and 36 guarantee personal liberty and fair hearing. These provisions inform our litigation, white collar defence and regulatory investigation practices at every stage.

Federal Structure & Legislative Competence

The Constitution's Exclusive and Concurrent Legislative Lists (Second Schedule) determine which matters fall to the National Assembly versus State Houses of Assembly — a distinction central to our real estate, taxation and regulatory advice.

Fundamental Rights & Property Protection

Section 44 protects against compulsory acquisition without prompt, adequate compensation — directly relevant to our real estate, mining and privatization mandates involving government-linked land or asset transactions.

Life at the Firm

Our Culture

Herger's & Co. invests deliberately in the professional development of every lawyer and support staff member, from structured mentorship for newly qualified associates to sponsored continuing legal education for partners tracking legislative change across their specialist areas.

We maintain a deliberately collegial, cross-practice culture: mandates spanning mining, taxation and compliance, or media, litigation and governance, are staffed by teams that consult across practice groups as a matter of course, rather than working in silos. This is, in our experience, the single greatest driver of the outcomes our clients value most.
