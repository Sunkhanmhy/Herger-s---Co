Careers & Positions

Build a specialist legal career at a firm that invests in depth, mentorship and direct client responsibility from your earliest years of practice.

Careers

Why Build Your Career at Herger's & Co.

Herger's & Co. hires deliberately and develops intentionally. Rather than rotating young lawyers across unrelated practice areas in their first years, we place each new associate within a specialist practice group early, pairing them with a partner mentor who takes direct responsibility for their technical development. This model produces lawyers who understand the specific statutory terrain of their practice area in genuine depth well before they reach senior associate level.

We also believe legal careers should reward substance over tenure alone. Associates at Herger's & Co. are given direct client contact far earlier than is typical at comparable firms, and our partnership track — detailed below — is explicitly available to any associate who demonstrates the technical and client-relationship capability the role requires, regardless of how long they have been at the firm.

Progression

The Partnership Membership Pathway

A transparent, merit-based track from Associate to Partner.

1

Associate

Placed within a specialist practice group from day one, working directly under a partner mentor on live client matters.

2

Senior Associate

Assumes primary day-to-day responsibility for matters within the practice group, with growing independent client contact.

3

Counsel

Leads significant mandates independently and begins direct business development responsibility within the practice group.

4

Partner

Admitted to the partnership upon demonstrated technical mastery, sustained client origination and a mentorship track record — see our full membership criteria on the Our Partners page.
