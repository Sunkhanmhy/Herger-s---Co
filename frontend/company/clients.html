Client Relationships

Our Clients - Our Priority!

Herger's & Co. measures success not by the number of matters opened in a year, but by how many clients return for their next matter, and how many refer the firm to their own commercial partners. Our current client base spans listed manufacturing and financial services groups, mid-tier mining and energy companies, international investors entering the Nigerian market, and a growing cohort of high-growth technology startups across fintech, logistics and media.

Because our practice groups are organised by specialist area rather than generalist teams, clients typically engage us initially for a single matter, such as - a tax dispute, a mining title acquisition, a fundraising round, then expand the relationship as they discover the firm's depth across adjacent practice areas. It is common for a client who first engaged our Capital Markets team for a bond issuance to later retain our Corporate Governance team for board advisory work, or our Litigation team when a commercial dispute arises.

Our client profiles and sector information below are illustrative of the type of organisations we serve; specific client names and confidential matter details are disclosed only with express client consent, consistent with our professional confidentiality obligations under the Rules of Professional Conduct for Legal Practitioners.

Our passion for succes and growth, with intents to satisfy our clients in the best way possible has been a major drive to having come this far. We remain committed to continuously improving our services and fostering long-term relationships with our clients.
