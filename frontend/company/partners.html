Our Partners

Twelve practice groups, one leadership philosophy: statutory precision, sector fluency and direct partner involvement in every mandate.

Leadership

Leadership Built on Deep Specialist Experience

Herger's & Co.'s partnership is structured so that every one of our twelve practice groups is led by a partner with sustained, current experience in that specific area of Nigerian law — not a generalist rotating across unrelated mandates. Each partner profiled below personally reviews and signs off on every significant matter in their practice group, and clients engaging the firm are introduced to the responsible partner from the first consultation, not only at matter closing.

Membership of the Herger's & Co. partnership is earned, not conferred by tenure alone: candidates for partnership must demonstrate not only technical excellence within their specialist area but a sustained record of direct client responsibility, business development and mentorship of the associates and team members who support them. This membership structure is described in detail below, alongside profiles of our senior associates and team members across each practice group.

Governance

How Partnership Membership Works at Herger's & Co.

Specialist Track Record

A minimum of eight years' sustained practice within a single specialist area, with direct client-facing responsibility for complex mandates.

Peer & Client Review

Candidacy requires endorsement from existing partners and documented client satisfaction across at least three significant matters.

Business Development Contribution

Demonstrated ability to originate and sustain client relationships independently, not solely execute matters referred by others.

Mentorship Commitment

An ongoing obligation to train and develop associates and team members within the partner's practice group.

Managing Partner — Litigation & Alternative Dispute Resolution
