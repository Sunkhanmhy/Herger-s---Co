About Herger's & Co.

Herger's & Co. was founded on a simple premise: that Nigerian businesses and the international investors who partners with us deserves legal counsel that is as rigorous about statutory detail as it is commercially pragmatic. Still in the course of our operations, we've partnered with prolific and senior level expertise in various legal sectors to further enhance our deliverables of justice, solicitation across all forms of legal practices.


Our practice is deliberately structured around twelve specialist areas of Nigerian law rather than a generalist model, because we believe depth produces better outcomes than breadth alone. Each practice group is led by partners who track legislative developments as they happen. Our express module of operation such as: the Finance Act amendments to Central Bank circulars and then translate them into practical guidance before they become compliance emergencies for our clients.
