Who We Are

Law & Justice

Herger's & Co. was founded on a simple premise: that individuals, families, corporate businesses and the international investors who partner with us deserves the best legal advice. We are a team of highly experienced legal counselors with keen determination for success and growth.

From the comfort of our head office, we provide our clients with top quality counsels ranging from the listed companies and multinational subsidiaries to family-owned enterprises and early-stage startups, across every stage of the corporate lifecycle, including formation, financing, growth, dispute and, where necessary, restructuring.
