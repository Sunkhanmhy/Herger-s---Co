Overview

Tracking the Regulatory Landscape as It Changes

Nigeria's legal and regulatory environment moves quickly — Finance Act amendments, new SEC rules, FCCPC merger guidelines and Central Bank circulars are issued throughout the year, often with immediate compliance implications. Our News & Events desk exists to keep clients and the wider public informed as these changes happen, translated from legislative text into practical guidance by the partner leading the relevant practice group.

Below you will find recent firm announcements, regulatory alerts and details of upcoming seminars and client briefings. For deeper analysis of specific legal topics, visit our Publications page.
